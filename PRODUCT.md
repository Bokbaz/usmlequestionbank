# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Medical students and graduates preparing for USMLE Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3. A deliberate mix of US MD/DO students (studying alongside school and rotations) and international medical graduates (often self-studying full time, value- and price-sensitive). Copy must read clearly to non-native English speakers; prices are in USD.

Typical scene: a third-year student in a library carrel mid-afternoon, or at a kitchen table at 11pm after a shift, grinding a 40-question timed block on a 13 to 15 inch laptop, then spending twice as long reviewing explanations. Tense and focused during the block, reflective during review, competitive when checking rank.

## Product Purpose

Argonaut USMLE is a question bank that competes head-on with UWorld, AMBOSS and Kaplan on the fundamentals (exam-faithful questions, clear explanations, performance analytics, a high-yield library) and wins on personalisation.

The paid differentiator is ARGO, Argonaut's proprietary analytics engine. ARGO measures everything a student does (accuracy, timing, answer changes, confidence, distractor choices, forgetting), models what they are weakest at, and keeps generating targeted question sets until the measured weakness is gone. The question bank becomes dynamic and tailored to each student.

Nuggets flag ultra-high-yield concepts. The free Daily Challenge (one brutal Step 1 question, timed, global leaderboard) is the acquisition funnel into accounts and paid plans.

Success looks like: students trust the questions, feel measurably sharper week over week, and can see exactly why.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, sharp, competitive. Elite training, not a classroom. Confident and precise like a top attending, with the energy of a scoreboard. Never cute, never hype. Every claim is backed by a number the student can inspect.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS: purple gradients, glowing blobs, glassmorphism, gradient text, identical icon-card grids.
- Gamified or childish: cartoon mascots, confetti everywhere, bouncy Duolingo motion.
- Dark neon "tech": black backgrounds, neon accents, terminal costume.
- Dated QBank UI: grey 2010-era test screens, cramped tables, clip-art icons.

## Design Principles

1. The exam is the benchmark. The test player mirrors real NBME ergonomics so practice transfers; nothing decorative competes with the vignette.
2. Show the evidence. Every insight ARGO gives is traceable to the attempts behind it; no black-box scores without the why.
3. Earn every pixel. Concise copy, dense where data demands it, generous where reading demands it.
4. Competitive by design. Ranks, percentiles, streaks and timing are first-class, presented with precision rather than celebration.
5. Calm in the block, alive at the edges. Motion and colour live on marketing and transitions; the testing surface stays still.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum. Light theme by default with a first-class dark theme for night study. Correct and incorrect states never rely on colour alone (icons and labels always accompany green and red). Full keyboard operation of the test player (A to E, N/P, M, Enter). `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured everywhere, including the marketing scroll choreography. Patient vignettes follow NBME guidance on varied, self-identified patient characteristics.
